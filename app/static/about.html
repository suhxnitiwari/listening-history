About me

Hi, I'm Suhani.

I study Management Information Systems at the McCombs School of Business at UT Austin, with a side of psychology, marketing and product strategy. I'm fascinated by why people choose what they choose, and it turns out my own music is a pretty good case study.

Wrapped gives me one year at a time. I wanted all four at once, so I treated my Spotify export like a real company's data: clean it, model it, load it into a database and ask it questions nobody had asked yet.
