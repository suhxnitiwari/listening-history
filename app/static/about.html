About me

Hi, I'm Suhani.

I study Management Information Systems at the McCombs School of Business at UT Austin, with a side of psychology, marketing and product strategy. I'm fascinated by why people choose what they choose, and it turns out my own music is a pretty good case study.

Wrapped gives me one year at a time. I wanted all four at once, so I treated my Spotify export like a real company's data: clean it, model it, load it into a database and ask it questions nobody had asked yet.

Why MIS

So why not English, Design, Studio Art, or Music Performance?

I read constantly, I write, I love playing instruments, and I've been making art for as long as I can remember. But the more I made, the more I noticed technology was already inside everything I loved: the tools I draw with, the apps that put stories in my hands, the playlist that finds a song before I know I want it. Someone had to understand people to build those things well.

That's what MIS is to me. It's the major for people who care as much about why someone uses a product as how it's built. I didn't leave the humanities behind. I brought them with me.

I learn enough technology to build it, enough business to know why we're building it, and enough about people to make sure what we build actually works for them.
