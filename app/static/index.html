            What I learned

            1. Cleaning decides the questions.Spotify files one song under several IDs: the single, the album, the deluxe. Until I merged them, my top songs were split across copies of themselves.
            2. Not every play is a listen.Twice, a song kept looping while I wasn't listening. Left in, one of those loops would have been my biggest single day ever. The 30-second rule and a loop filter keep the data honest.
            3. Having a field isn't a reason to keep it.The export tracks where and on what I listened. None of my questions needed it, so none of it was kept.
