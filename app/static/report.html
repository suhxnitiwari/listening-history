                      Suhani Tiwari, Listening History

                      How the receipts were made

                      • Extract: Python reads Spotify's extended streaming history, every play since May 2022.
                      • Clean: songs only, Austin time, duplicates removed, one song released under many IDs merged into one, plays grouped into sessions.
                      • Model: a star schema in PostgreSQL: one fact table of plays and five dimensions (song, artist, album, day, session).
                      • Ask: SQL with CTEs and window functions (gaps and islands for streaks, LAG for comebacks and silences, lift for 2 AM me), served by FastAPI.

                      What I left out on purpose

                      • IP address, country and device: dropped before anything is saved. Private sessions: excluded.
                      • Plays I didn't really make: "Party In The U.S.A." looping overnight (March 21, 2023), "Hate Me" doing the same (January 22, 2025), and every play of "How Far I'll Go" (April 2023), an accident.
                      • Raw export files: never on GitHub.

                      The judgment calls

                      • Moods are my own labels for my 389 most-played songs (61.5% of my listens): heartbreak (−2), bittersweet and dark (−1), love and confident (+1), party (+2). A day's weather is the average of its labeled listens (10 or more); "saddest-sounding" needs 40.
                      • Desi means South Asian music, Hindi, Punjabi or Urdu, from my own list of 87 artists in my top 300.
                      • Eras split at my graduation (June 1, 2024) and my move to Austin (August 15, 2024).
                      • 2 AM me uses lift: how much more likely an artist is in that part of the day than across my whole day (artists with 150+ listens).

                      Definitions

                      ListenA play of 30 seconds or more, Spotify's own rule for a stream. Charts count listens unless they say plays.
                      SkipA play I moved past with the forward button or quit before 30 seconds.
                      StreakDays in a row with at least one listen of the same song.
                      All-nighterA night with music in every hour from midnight to 6 AM.
                      ComebackA song with 40+ listens, then 180+ days of silence, then 25+ listens within 60 days.

                      Where it stops being evidence

                      • A play means the song was on, not that I was listening, awake or feeling it. A sad song isn't proof of a sad day.
                      • Spotify only: no YouTube, radio or someone else's aux. 2022 starts in late May and 2026 stops in September.
